Our most used screen started as three support tickets.
Nobody flagged them. Nobody asked for a new feature.
Each ticket used different words, and all three wanted the same thing: to see every open order on one page instead of clicking through five tabs.

I almost filed them under "nice to have". Then I put them side by side on a Friday afternoon and read them again.

One customer called it "the big list". One asked for "an export, but on the screen". One wrote a long paragraph that ended with "just show me everything".

We built a plain table in two weeks. No charts, no filters at launch. Six months later it is the first screen most accounts open in the morning, and the tabs it replaced get a fraction of the traffic.

What I changed after this: once a month I read the last fifty tickets in one sitting, out of order, with the product names blacked out. The feature requests are rarely where the pattern is. The workarounds are.

What does your support queue keep asking for in words that never make it onto the roadmap?
